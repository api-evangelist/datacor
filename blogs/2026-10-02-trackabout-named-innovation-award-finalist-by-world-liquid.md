FLORHAM PARK NJ, October 2, 2026 -- Datacor today announced that TrackAbout, its cylinder inventory management & tracking platform, has been selected as one of 10 finalists for the Innovation Award at the World Liquid Gas Association (WLGA) Global Technology Conference (GTC). The GTC takes place during Liquid Gas Week 2026, held October 12 to 16 in Istanbul, Türkiye, under the theme "Resilience in a Changing World."
