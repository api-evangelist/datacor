Florham Park, NJ, September 17, 2026 – Datacor, Inc., a leading provider of process manufacturing, chemical distribution, and engineering software, today announced its Summer 2026 Product Release, a set of more than 50 enhancements across its portfolio that automate manual document and data handling, connect information across the business, and build compliance into everyday operations. Together, the updates move customers from data entry toward faster, better-informed decisions, from the plant floor to the front office.
